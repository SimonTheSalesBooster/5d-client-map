# 5D Client Map

**Most B2B content is about the seller. Make yours about the buyer, and start the conversations you want.**
A free Claude Code skill that checks every post, email and DM against what your ideal client wants, before it goes out.

Build your map once. Paste any draft. Get PASS or FIX on each of the 5 D's, plus the rebuilt draft.

You want the right founders replying. Bigger deals at the price you want. Fewer deals where you're the only one who can close. 🐬

So you post. Clean hook, tight copy. Your peers like it.
And the buyer you actually want scrolls straight past.

Think of the best deal you ever closed. Chances are the buyer felt understood before you pitched a thing.

The copy isn't the problem. The target is.
The market is flooded with posts about the method, the offer, the new feature. Buyers only stop for their own world.

Miss their world and even the replies start behind: you explaining, them doubting.
Hit it and they arrive already nodding, quoting your own words back to you. You lead the call from the first minute. 🐯

This skill fixes the target before you polish the copy. Every draft goes out fresh, aimed at one buyer.

## The 5 D's

**1. Define: who they are.** Who signs, what size, what trigger makes them buy *right now*. And who it is NOT for.

**2. Desire: what they say they want.** In their words. This is your hook, your headline, your subject line.

**3. Deficit: what is really missing.** The thing that stops them getting what they want. Pick the ONE that matters most (the yellow marker).

**4. Deliver: what you bring.** Every offer closes a deficit. If one doesn't, question why you sell it.

**5. Delight: what they remember.** The moments clients tell their friends about. That's your proof.

Chew on this rule of thumb 🐯
> Clients buy what they **Desire**. You win and keep them by fixing the **Deficit**. They refer you because of the **Delight**.

Market to the Desire. Surface the Deficit in the conversation. Design the Delight on purpose.

## Install

```bash
git clone https://github.com/SimonTheSalesBooster/5d-client-map
cp 5d-client-map/5d-client-map.md ~/.claude/commands/
```

**Build your map (once):**
```
/5d-client-map build
```
Claude interviews you, one question at a time, and writes `5d-client-map.md`.

**Check any draft (every time):**
```
/5d-client-map
[paste your post / email / DM / landing page]
```

You get:
```
5D CHECK: FIX
Define   PASS  founder-owner, B2B, hiring sales
Desire   FIX   hook opens on our method, not on "stop being the only closer"
Deficit  PASS  control of the sales conversation (yellow marker)
Deliver  FIX   post raises "no repeatable process", offers a newsletter
Delight  PASS  first call where they lead and the buyer says yes
Stage    start a conversation -> reply "READY"
```
...plus the rebuilt draft with every fix applied. 🌴

Map once. Check every draft. It becomes the habit before you hit post.

Run it FIRST, before any copywriting check. Polishing copy that's off-target only gives you better copy about the wrong thing.
