# Example: the Strategy Sprints 5D Client Map

This is the real map we run every piece of our own content, email and DM through. Use it as a reference for how specific yours should be.


Every piece of content, campaign, offer and conversation should be built from this map.
**Rule of thumb:** clients buy what they **Desire**. We win and keep them by fixing the **Deficit**. They refer us because of the **Delight**.
Market to the Desire. Surface the Deficit in the conversation. Design the Delight on purpose.

### 1. DEFINE: who they are
- Founder / CEO / Owner (owns the company, never a hired executive)
- B2B: Software, Consulting, Financial Services
- ~$3-6M revenue, wants to break $10M
- Hiring salespeople RIGHT NOW (live sales-role posting, last 90 days)
- Tracks their own performance (Strava, races, Whoop, Garmin)
- Coachable: already uses coaches, masterminds, EO / YPO / Vistage
- US, DACH, Western Europe (also Singapore, UAE, Japan)

**NOT FOR (anti-ICP):** technical / HR / finance roles; not hiring sales; never worked with any coach; B2C; healthcare, defense, public sector; big corporates; revenue under ~$1.2M.

### 2. DESIRE: what they say they want (use their words in headlines, hooks, subject lines)
- Double revenue, fast ("Don't grow slow")
- Stop being the only closer
- New sales hires who actually perform
- Bigger deals at the price they want
- Time back: evenings, weekends, family
- Live the adventure entrepreneurship promised

### 3. DEFICIT: what is really missing (surface it in the conversation, don't lead ads with it)
- **CONTROL of the sales conversation (no begging, no neediness)** (the yellow-marker insight: the single most important line on the map)
- A repeatable process a new hire can run without them
- The right things in the right order
- Spotters who push them through their current sales wall
- A weekly rhythm to measure, learn, adjust
- Pricing power

### 4. DELIVER: what we bring (every item closes a Deficit)
- **The 8 Steps of the Repeatable Sale:** 1 Visualize, 2 Frustration, 3 Importance, 4 Cost of Inaction, 5 Deliverables, 6 Investment, 7 Starting Date, 8 Statement of Work (closes: control, right order)
- **Monday Strategy + Friday Learnings:** set the week Monday, score it Friday (closes: weekly rhythm)
- **Jetpack Workshop** (1 day): morning see what's possible, afternoon start implementing
- **90-Day Sales Acceleration:** AI-powered sales + marketing processes installed (closes: process a new hire can run)
- **200K Club / Custom Team Coaching:** the spotters
- **Sprint Club:** tools, AI skills, community

### 5. DELIGHT: what they remember (and tell friends about)
- Personal welcome DM from Simon on day 1
- Workshop: the AI advisors build the day LIVE in front of them
- The Statement of Work is written in THEIR words
- The first call where they lead, and the buyer says yes
- The Friday scorecard shows the win in black and white
- A high-energy battle finale closes every coaching
- Revenue doubled in 90 days

### The outcome under the map
**Sprintlife:** control in sales + time freedom = money, thank-yous and purpose.
